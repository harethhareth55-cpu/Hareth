# Toast

A short dark message at the top of the screen confirming an action or reporting why it failed.

One sentence, past tense for success (تم تنزيل الإكسل), and for errors what went wrong and what to do (مكتبة المسح ما تحمّلت. حدّث الصفحة وأنت متصل مرة واحدة.). It ignores taps and disappears on its own.
